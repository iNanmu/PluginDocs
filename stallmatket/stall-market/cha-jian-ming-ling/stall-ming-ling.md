# Stall 命令

<details>

<summary>Open</summary>

/stall open

打开插件主界面 (main.yml)

</details>

<details>

<summary>Market</summary>

/stall market

打开摊位市场界面 (market.yml)

</details>

<details>

<summary>Self</summary>

/stall self

打开玩家自身摊位列表界面 (stall\_list.yml)

</details>

<details>

<summary>Warehouse</summary>

/stall warehouse

打开摊位仓库界面 (warehouse.yml)

</details>

<details>

<summary>Reload</summary>

/stall reload

重载插件

</details>
